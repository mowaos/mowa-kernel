    Time travel

    Every step is kept. Drag the slider to look at any earlier step, rewind to branch from it, or tamper with it to see verification fail.

    Rewinding replays the kept steps on a fresh host. The hashes come out identical, because the kernel reads no clock, randomness or floats.

    Hash audit

    Fingerprints (SHA-256 hashes) of the selected step, computed twice: by the kernel in WebAssembly, and by a separate JavaScript implementation of the published specification. They must match.

    FieldWASM kernelJS spec hasher
